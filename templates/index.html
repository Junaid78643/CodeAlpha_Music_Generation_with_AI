How AuraTone AI Composes Music

01

MIDI Ingestion

Parsed authentic Bach, Beethoven chorales and Joplin jazz progressions using music21 & mido.

02

Token Preprocessing

Notes, chords, and rhythmic durations quantized into structured tokens with sliding 16-step window frames.

03

Deep LSTM Network

Multi-gate recurrent neural network with Adam optimization learns temporal dependencies and harmonic cadence.

04

Harmonic Synthesis

Autoregressive temperature sampling decodes tokens into Standard MIDI (.mid) and master 44.1kHz WAV audio.
